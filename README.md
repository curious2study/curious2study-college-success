# College Success - Curious2Study

## Course Overview

This course is designed for college students seeking to maximize their academic performance and overall college experience. Whether you're navigating your first semester or looking to improve as an upperclassman, this course provides strategies for thriving in college.

## Course Philosophy

College success isn't about perfection—it's about continuous improvement and intentional decision-making. This course teaches you to be a strategic learner and develop resilience for college and beyond.

## Course Materials

Each section includes:
- **Video Lectures:** Strategies and real-world examples
- **Study Guides:** Key concepts and implementation steps

## How to Use This Course

1. Watch the video lectures for areas where you need support
2. Read the study guides to reinforce concepts
3. Apply strategies to your college experience

## Learning Approach

This course emphasizes:
- Practical application of strategies
- Student autonomy and choice
- Continuous reflection and adjustment
- Celebrating progress and growth
